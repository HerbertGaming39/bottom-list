THE BOTTOM LIST Guidelines

By using this site, submitting records, or submitting levels, you agree to follow these rules.

1. General behavior

  • Be respectful to all users, staff, and creators.
  • No harassment, hate speech, or targeted abuse.
  • No impersonation of staff or other players.

2. Record submissions

  • Submit only your own completions.
  • Proof must be clear and must match the selected level.
  • Fake, edited, or misleading proof may lead to penalties.
  • All ratings are required: Deco, Gameplay, and Enjoyment.

3. Level submissions

  • Only submit legitimate level data.
  • Do not spam duplicate or low-effort submissions.
  • Provide accurate metadata (creator, ID, and verification details).

4. Enforcement

  • Staff may reject submissions that do not meet standards.
  • Repeated abuse can result in warnings, restrictions, or bans.
  • Staff decisions are final unless explicitly reviewed.
