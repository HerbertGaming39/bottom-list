Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2025

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: Username, email (if provided), hashed password, Discord ID (if linked).
  • Activity data: Level records, submissions, comments, and site interactions.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser user-agent, and cookies for site functionality.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate and maintain the Bottom List leaderboard and community features.
  • To verify records and prevent cheating or abuse.
  • To communicate with you via Discord or site notifications.
  • To improve site experience and fix bugs.

3. Cookies

This site uses minimal cookies for essential functionality:

  • Session cookie: Keeps you logged in.
  • UI preference cookie: Remembers your chosen theme.
  • Consent cookie: Remembers your cookie preference.

No tracking or advertising cookies are used. You can decline all non-essential cookies.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Public profile information (username, records, submissions) is visible to all site visitors as part of the leaderboard service.

5. Data Retention

Account data is retained until you request deletion. Records and submissions are kept indefinitely for leaderboard integrity. Contact an admin to request data removal.

6. Contact

For privacy concerns, contact a site admin via the Discord server.
