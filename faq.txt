================================================================================
                        THE BOTTOM LIST — FAQ
================================================================================

1. GENERAL
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q: What is The Bottom List & how do I join?
A: A GD level list with Main/Challenge/Platformer lists. Register at
   /register (username, 8+ char password), or use "Log in with Discord".
   Forgot password? /forgot-password. Delete account from profile page.


2. LEVELS & LISTS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q: How do I browse levels?
A: Homepage = Main list. /challenges and /platformer for other lists.
   Filter by tags (Ship, Wave, Timings, etc.). /unverified shows
   levels pending verification. Click any level for full details (position,
   creator, video, description, song, records, ratings).


3. RECORDS & SUBMISSIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q: How do I submit records or levels?
A: /submit/record — select level, rate it (Deco/Gameplay/Difficulty), provide
   proof (top levels = video, lower = screenshot, platformer = always video).
   /submit/level — propose a new level for the list. Staff review all
   submissions; approved records earn points.


4. POINTS & LEADERBOARDS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q: How do points and rankings work?
A: Beating levels earns points (higher-ranked = more). /leaderboard shows
   rankings sortable by points/records/activity, filterable by device/status.
   Clan leaderboard toggle available. Points tracked per list.


5. PROFILES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q: What profile features are there?
A: Each profile shows rank, points, avatar, banner, bio, YouTube, Discord,
   completion stats, recent activity. Customize at /profile/edit. Full stats
   dashboard at /user/{id}/stats. Change username at /profile/change_username.
   Export all data as JSON at /me/export.


6. UI & THEMES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q: Can I customize the site appearance?
A: Yes. Choose Nova UI or Aurora UI on first visit (change in Settings).
   Custom accent color available. UI Templates from staff/marketplace in
   Settings → UI Theme. Toggle thumbnails off in Settings.


7. SHOP, B-COINS & WHEEL
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q: Tell me about the economy.
A: B-Coins are the currency (start with 50). Earn more from the Wheel of
   Fortune (/wheel — spin daily for coins or rare items) or resell on the
   Black Market (/black-market — list limited items min 1000 coins). Spend
   on cosmetics at /shop (avatar animations, backgrounds, decorations).
   View/equip items at /inventory.


8. ROULETTE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q: How does roulette work?
A: Single-player: /roulette — configurable random level picker. Multiplayer:
   create/join rooms with a 6-char code, spin levels, submit results
   (first-try %, best %, attempts). Browse public rooms from roulette page.


9. CLANS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q: How do clans work?
A: /clans — browse or create your own. Send join requests (one clan at a
   time). Owners accept/deny members, kick, edit description, or disband.
   Leave anytime; owners must disband to leave.


10. MAP PACKS & CUSTOM LISTS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q: What are Map Packs and Custom Lists?
A: Map Packs are curated level collections at /map-packs (shows completion %).
   Custom Lists are user-created collections at /custom-lists.


11. EVENTS & NEWS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q: Where are events and news?
A: Events at /events (time-limited). Latest 5 news posts on homepage.


12. YEAR-IN-REVIEW
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q: What is the Recap page?
A: /recap — annual summary of levels beaten, points, monthly breakdowns,
   device usage, top levels, streaks, ratings, global rank, rivals, clan stats.


13. API
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q: Is there an API?
A: Yes at /api/* — levels, players, clans, events, leaderboards, etc.
   Get your token from Settings or GET /api/token. Docs at /api/docs
   (login required).


14. PRIVACY & COOKIES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q: What about privacy and security?
A: Essential session cookies only (login, CSRF) — Accept/Decline on banner.
   Full details at /privacy. HTTPS (Let's Encrypt), hashed passwords,
   regeneratable API tokens.
